AKAN NAME GENERATOR

Akan names originate from the Akan people who live in Ghana and is tradinationally based on the day of the week you wereborn, with different names for males and females.The names are believed to carry spritual meaning linked to personality, traits and personality. they help know the day you were born and the expected traits to have. They are important because they help us connect birth and destiny they also give us social meaning and helps the Akan people preserve traditions.
